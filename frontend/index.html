Caution Safety Screener haiku-4-5 · flags, never diagnoses

One flag (caution): the long-run mileage bump loads a historically flagged knee. Proceed, but ease in and stop if it flares — see a physio if it lingers. Nothing else trips a rule; calories, load, and recovery are all within safe bounds.

This is coaching guidance, not medical advice. Check with a healthcare professional before big changes, and stop anything that causes pain.
